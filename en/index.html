How it works

The game above the fold, your pencil below.

1
Read the prompt
The game appears on the top half, above the fold, with no gesture needed.
2
Write your answer
With your finger or a stylus, on the grid slate across the bottom half.
3
Plio reacts
Right, or not quite, with the correct answer. Then on to the next exercise.
Today's session

Four exercises, one session of about six minutes.

Speed Math
2 min · 20 problems

Addition, subtraction, times tables, and division, getting trickier as you go. Answers are always whole numbers.

Stroop
1 min · 12 words

Tap the color of the ink, not the written word. Each color also has its own shape.

Digit Memory
2 min · 8 sequences

Remember a sequence of 3 to 7 digits, then write it back from memory.

Mirror
75 s · 6 patterns

Copy the pattern as a mirror image on the other side of the fold, as if you were folding the screen over.

On your devices

Designed for the screen that folds.

iPhone Duo

Open: the game above the fold, the slate below. Folded to 90° and set down: the top screen becomes a blackboard and Plio sits on the edge. Closed: everything fits on the outer screen.

iPhone

The game at the top of the screen, the slate at the bottom, right under your thumb. With no fold, the screen simply splits in two.

iPad + Apple Pencil

The game on the left, a large slate on the right for writing with a stylus.
