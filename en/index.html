Today's session

Four families, one exercise from each, changing every day.

Math

Speed Math

Attention

Stroop · Sorting

Memory

Digit Memory · Marbles

Remember a sequence of digits and write it back. Or follow a marble through switches that flip.

Space

Mirror · Views · Scales

Copy a pattern as a mirror image across the fold, find the view of a stack of cubes, order objects by weight.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. Fold Brain is fully featured on any iPhone and iPad: without a fold, the screen simply splits in two. The Duo adds desk mode and games that cross the fold.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sessions, handwriting, and the reminder all work offline. You only need a connection to buy, restore a purchase, or send scores to Game Center.

Do I need an Apple Pencil on iPad?

No. Writing with your finger works just as well, or switch to the number pad from the toolbar.

New device: how do I get my purchase back?

Go to Settings > Purchase > Restore Purchase with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is included.

What happens to my data?

Your sessions stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. The details are in the privacy policy.
