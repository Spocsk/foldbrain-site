Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2026

In short

Fold Brain collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. Only your purchase history goes through our payment provider, RevenueCat, under an anonymous identifier.

What stays on your iPhone

Your sessions, brain age, streak, stamps, and settings are stored only on this device. They are sent neither to us nor to any third party, except the scores you share with Game Center (see below). Deleting the app or using “Reset progress” erases them.

Handwriting

What you write on the slate is read directly on the iPhone by Apple's text recognition. Your strokes are neither stored nor sent anywhere.

Purchase

The “Fold Brain, Forever” purchase is paid through Apple's App Store, then verified by RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), which receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier created by the app. This identifier is not linked to your name, your email, or your payment details, which we never receive. It is used only to unlock the app and restore your purchase.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends it your brain age, best streak, and achievements for the leaderboards. Game Center is an Apple service with its own privacy policy; we have no access to your account. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your iPhone. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.

Children

Fold Brain collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

This site

This site uses no cookies and no audience measurement tools. It is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your language choice is remembered in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page with its update date.

Contact

Questions? Write to us at apps@dylan-cdo.fr.
